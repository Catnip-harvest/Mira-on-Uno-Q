An SO-101 robot arm carries a camera. You steer it from a phone in XR; it scans, inspects, records and replays trajectories over real objects.

Close-up inspection with no cloud, no GPU, no connectivity — every frame stays on the 2 GB board. Private by physics, not by policy.
