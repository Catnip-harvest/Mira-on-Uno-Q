02 · PROBLEM → PRODUCT

Close-up inspection is manual, hazardous, or cloud-bound

PROBLEM.

Inspecting objects up close means hands, ladders or expensive rigs — and the smart-camera products that exist stream your video to someone else’s cloud.

PRODUCT.

Mira: an SO-101 arm carries a camera you steer from a phone in XR. It scans, inspects, records and replays trajectories.

THE TWIST.

Control, capture and the UI itself run on one 2 GB UNO Q. No GPU, no cloud — nothing leaves the device.

NOTICE — SAFETY IS A FEATURE

Board power-off does not stop the arm — servos freeze rigid. So the MCU alone gates the 12 V rail:

heartbeat stale → rail drops
E-stop opens → rail drops
corrupt frame → rail drops
faults latch → a human resets

Linux cannot override it. We demo this live.
